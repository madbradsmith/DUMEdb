Platform Standards

Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 • Applies To: Website & DUMEdb software • Status: Draft pending counsel review
DRAFT: this document is a working draft and has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel.
Principle:
DUMEdb is built to give AI agents reliable, auditable memory. Use it lawfully, do not use it to harm people or systems, and do not misuse the website or the project's infrastructure.
01

Permitted Use

  • Running DUMEdb locally to give your own AI agents and workflows persistent, structured memory.
  • Building, testing, modifying and redistributing the software as allowed by its license.
  • Evaluating, benchmarking and writing about the project.
02

Prohibited Use

  • Unlawful activity: using the software or website to violate any applicable law or regulation, or to infringe another party's rights.
  • Harm to people: using it to harass, defraud, stalk, or build systems intended to deceive or cause physical, financial or psychological harm.
  • Unauthorized data: storing personal data you have no right to hold, or using agents to collect it without authorization.
  • Attacks on infrastructure: denial-of-service, abusive automated scraping, vulnerability probing without permission, or attempts to compromise the website, hosting or distribution channels.
  • Malware and tampering: distributing modified builds that are presented as official but contain malicious or deceptive code.
  • Impersonation and false claims: implying endorsement, certification or compliance (including SOC 2) that does not exist, or misrepresenting the project's origin or capabilities.
03

Your Agents Are Your Responsibility

If you connect an AI agent to DUMEdb, you are responsible for the actions that agent takes and the content it writes. Automated activity must follow this policy and the terms of any third-party service the agent uses.

04

Enforcement

Because the software runs on your machine and we do not monitor your use of it, enforcement is limited to what we control: we may remove content, restrict access to the website or project channels, report unlawful activity to authorities, and withdraw support. Misuse may also expose you to legal liability under applicable law.

05

Reporting Abuse and Vulnerabilities

To report misuse of the project or its brand, or to responsibly disclose a security issue, contact:

Abuse reports: abuse@expeditum.dev
Security disclosures: security@expeditum.dev
