Local-First Data Handling Specification

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 • Scope: dumedb.developumaiengine.com & the DUMEdb software • Status: Draft pending counsel review
DRAFT: this document is a working draft and has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel.
Plain-English summary:
This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics scripts. The DUMEdb software runs on your own machine and stores its registers locally. We do not receive them. The one thing to understand: if you point DUMEdb at a hosted AI model, whatever you send to that model is governed by that provider, not by us.
01

Two Different Things Covered Here

This policy covers (a) this public website and (b) the open-source DUMEdb software you can download and run yourself. They behave very differently, so they are described separately below.

02

This Website

  • No accounts or forms: the site is static content. It has no sign-up, login, contact form or payment flow.
  • No cookies or browser storage: as of the effective date the site's own code sets no cookies and does not use localStorage or sessionStorage.
  • No analytics or advertising tags: we do not load analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts.
  • Web fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page. You can block that request in your browser and the site will fall back to system fonts.
  • Hosting logs: our hosting provider (Hostinger) may keep standard server logs such as IP address, user agent and timestamps for security and operations. We do not use these logs to profile visitors.
03

The DUMEdb Software (Runs on Your Machine)

  • Local storage: registers, the Cabinet and session state are stored in a local database on the computer where you run DUMEdb. By default the engine listens on the loopback address (127.0.0.1) and the Inspector is served locally.
  • No data sent to us: the software does not transmit your registers to us. We operate no server that receives them, and we have no ability to read, recover or delete your local data.
  • You control retention: you can inspect, edit and delete registers yourself. Deleting the local data directory removes it.
04

AI Models and Third-Party Endpoints

Some DUMEdb features (for example the proxy and the synthesis features) can send text to a language-model endpoint that you configure. By default this points to a model on your own machine (localhost). If you configure a hosted provider, the content you send is processed under that provider's terms and privacy policy, and any API key you supply is used to authenticate to that provider. We do not control those providers and are not responsible for how they handle your data.

The same applies to the AI assistant or IDE you connect to DUMEdb through MCP: what that assistant reads from DUMEdb and sends onward is governed by the assistant's provider.

05

No Sale or Brokerage of Data

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • We run no advertising on the website and have no data-broker relationships.
06

Security and Compliance Status

We are working toward a SOC 2 Type II examination. That audit is in progress and has not been completed; no SOC 2 report has been issued. Nothing on this site should be read as a statement that a SOC 2 attestation exists. Because the software is local-first, much of its security posture depends on how you secure your own machine, accounts and API keys.

07

Children

The website and software are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

08

Changes to This Policy

If our practices change (for example if analytics or accounts are ever added) we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
